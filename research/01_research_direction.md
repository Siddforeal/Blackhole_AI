# Research Direction: Blackhole_AI

Status: planning only, not a paper draft.

## Working direction

Evidence-gated human-in-the-loop AI assistance for vulnerability research planning.

## Core idea

Blackhole_AI should not be studied as an autonomous vulnerability scanner.

The first research direction should study whether a structured, safety-gated, human-in-the-loop workflow can help security researchers improve:

- investigation planning
- evidence completeness
- validation readiness
- blocker identification
- safety discipline
- responsible disclosure readiness

## What this research is not claiming yet

This research does not claim that Blackhole_AI:

- automatically finds vulnerabilities
- replaces human researchers
- discovers more critical bugs than humans
- is better than commercial scanners
- safely performs autonomous exploitation
- is complete or production-ready

## First paper goal

The first paper should evaluate one stable part of Blackhole_AI:

A workflow that turns messy vulnerability-research context into structured hypotheses, evidence checklists, validation plans, safety blockers, and report-readiness decisions.

## Current decision

Do not write the paper yet.

First build the research foundation, define research questions, design evaluation, create controlled cases, and only later write based on real results.
